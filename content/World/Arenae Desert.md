The Arenae Desert, a great nation of clans lead by The Royal Family. Ruling from the capital of [[Gilbralter]], a city of some size, on the Eastern side of the Arenae Desert. Holding full control of the Southern coast of the [[Predoris Sea]] while sharing control of the Flumex river with their Northern neighbors, the Royal family holds control of the only source of clean drinking water in the entire Arenae Desert. Fearing the same outcome of their ancestors from [[Tan Dortonus]], the Royal Family has outlawed the use of magic within the capital, and limited its use with it's nations boarders. Within the clans are a small group of rebels who think that magicitech could be a useful resource. Remaining hidden from the Loyalist and the Royal family they work to bring them down. Using the valuable ores and minerals caused by [[The Cataclysm]], both sides fund their war efforts.
